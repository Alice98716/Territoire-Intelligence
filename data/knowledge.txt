-- POLICY A: OFFICE SNACKS --
The company provides free fruit on Mondays and Thursdays. 
On Fridays, there is a "Pizza Social" at 4:00 PM in the breakroom. 
Employees must log their dietary restrictions in the HR portal by Wednesday.

-- POLICY B: TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT --
Employees can claim up to $50 per day for meals during business trips. 
Travelers must use the "Expensify" app to upload receipts. 
International travel requires approval from the Department Head 30 days in advance.

-- POLICY C: COMPUTER EQUIPMENT --
Standard issue laptops are replaced every 3 years. 
If a screen breaks, employees should contact "Tech-Support-Joe" on Slack. 
Home office setups (chairs/desks) are eligible for a one-time $300 stipend.
